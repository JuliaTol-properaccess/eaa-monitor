ACM supervision

Do Dutch travel companies have an accessibility statement?

Every Monday this monitor checks the footer of major Dutch travel companies, from tour operators to online travel agencies, for a link to an accessibility statement. Anyone who sells travel online provides an e-commerce service and falls under the EAA. The ACM supervises this.

Important: an accessibility statement is a promise, not proof. Whether a website really works for people with a disability only shows in a substantive test of the site itself. This monitor only checks whether the statement is in the footer, not whether it is accurate or how accessible the site really is.

European infrastructure

This website runs entirely on European services. The servers are at Hetzner in Germany, the domain runs through SIDN in the Netherlands, the visitor statistics come from Plausible in Europe and we send email with AhaSend from the Netherlands. Your visit touches no US service.

Good to know

The check runs weekly. A link to a statement doesn't automatically mean a website is actually accessible.
